# PseudoAI Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 5 September 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how PseudoAI processes personal data when you use PseudoAI, including the PseudoAI plugin for ChatGPT and Codex, its user interface, related MCP services and associated PseudoAI services (together, the “Service”).

The Service is provided by **Dutchhamburgers**, trading as PseudoAI (“PseudoAI”, “we”, “us” or “our”), established at **Vierbanse Gantel 21, Tilburg**, the Netherlands, Chamber of Commerce number **42032983**.

PseudoAI is designed around data minimization. Its purpose is to help users identify and reduce personal, confidential or otherwise sensitive information before information is intentionally shared with an external AI service.

## 1. How PseudoAI works

Depending on the configuration provided by your organisation, PseudoAI can analyse text and documents, identify potentially sensitive information, allow that information to be reviewed, and replace selected information with tokens, pseudonyms or other minimized representations.

PseudoAI is designed so that original information can be processed within a protected environment controlled by the user’s organisation.

In the standard organisation-controlled deployment, documents, prompts, original values and other information processed for detection and pseudonymization remain within the organisation’s PseudoAI environment unless the user or organisation explicitly decides to submit information to an external service.

PseudoAI does not require original sensitive information to be provided to us as the provider of the software in order to perform normal detection and pseudonymization functions.

## 2. The boundary between PseudoAI and ChatGPT

PseudoAI and ChatGPT/OpenAI are separate processing environments.

PseudoAI is intended to reduce the information made available to an external AI service. Original values that are replaced by PseudoAI should remain within the protected PseudoAI environment, while only the minimized or pseudonymized information approved for submission is made available to ChatGPT or another selected AI service.

Where pseudonymization is used, the information required to associate a token with its original value is maintained within the applicable protected PseudoAI environment and under the control of the relevant organisation.

The public PseudoAI plugin and its MCP interface are not intended to use ChatGPT as a transport mechanism for original unminimized sensitive information.

Users should review the information presented for submission before approving its transmission to ChatGPT or another external AI service.

## 3. Personal data that may be processed

The categories of data processed depend on how PseudoAI is configured and used.

### 3.1 Content selected by the user

A user may choose to process text, documents or other content using PseudoAI. Such content may contain names, contact details, organisation-specific information, case information, financial information or other information that the organisation considers confidential or sensitive.

This content is processed for the purpose of detecting, classifying, highlighting, masking or pseudonymizing information.

In the standard organisation-controlled deployment, this processing takes place within the organisation’s PseudoAI environment.

### 3.2 Pseudonyms and tokens

PseudoAI may replace selected information with tokens or pseudonyms.

A token by itself is designed not to reveal the original value. Where a token can be linked back to a person using separately held mapping information, the token may still constitute personal data under applicable data-protection law.

Token mappings are maintained separately from the information submitted to an external AI service.

### 3.3 Account and authentication information

Where authentication is enabled, PseudoAI may process limited account information required to provide authorised access to the Service. This may include a user identifier, organisation identifier, role or access entitlement and authentication-related technical information.

Authentication secrets such as passwords, API keys, MFA codes or one-time passwords must not be included in text or documents submitted to external AI services through PseudoAI.

### 3.4 Operational and audit information

Where enabled by the organisation, PseudoAI may generate operational or audit information necessary for security, administration and accountability.

PseudoAI is designed to minimize such information. Audit information should not contain document content or original sensitive values unless this is explicitly configured and necessary.

### 3.5 Support information

PseudoAI does not receive customer documents, prompts or datasets as part of the standard operation of the Service.

If an organisation requests support or maintenance that requires access to personal data, such access will take place only for the specific support purpose, for a limited period and subject to appropriate security and confidentiality measures.

## 4. Restricted and highly sensitive information

The PseudoAI plugin is designed to help prevent unnecessary disclosure of sensitive information to external AI services.

The PseudoAI MCP interface is not intended to collect or solicit payment-card information, protected health information, government identification numbers, passwords, API keys, authentication secrets, MFA codes or one-time passwords.

Such information must not intentionally be provided as plugin or MCP input.

Where an organisation processes such information within its own protected PseudoAI environment for the purpose of identifying and removing it before external transmission, the original information should remain within that controlled environment and must not be transmitted to ChatGPT or another external AI provider through the PseudoAI MCP interface.

## 5. Purposes of processing

PseudoAI processes information only where necessary for purposes connected with providing and securing the Service.

These purposes include:

- detecting potentially sensitive information;
- classifying information according to configured policies;
- presenting information to users for review;
- masking or pseudonymizing selected information;
- enabling re-identification within the protected environment where applicable;
- authenticating authorised users;
- maintaining security and access controls;
- generating appropriately minimized audit information;
- providing support at the organisation’s request;
- complying with applicable legal obligations.

PseudoAI does not determine which documents an organisation processes, why those documents are processed or which external AI services an organisation chooses to use.

## 6. User control

PseudoAI is designed to provide users with control over information before it is intentionally submitted to an external AI service.

Depending on the configuration selected by the organisation, users may be able to:

- review detected information;
- assess warnings;
- change classifications;
- add or remove masking;
- review pseudonymized information;
- decide whether information should be submitted.

PseudoAI assists users with data minimization but automated detection is inherently imperfect. PseudoAI cannot guarantee that every item of personal, confidential or sensitive information will be detected.

Users should therefore perform a reasonable final review before approving information for transmission to an external AI service.

## 7. Who receives personal data

In the standard organisation-controlled deployment, original customer content is processed within the environment controlled by the organisation and is not routinely disclosed to PseudoAI as software provider.

Information may be made available to the following categories of recipients where applicable:

**The user’s organisation.** The organisation controls the PseudoAI environment, user access, policies and configuration.

**External AI providers.** Information is provided to ChatGPT/OpenAI or another AI provider only when the applicable workflow intentionally submits that information. PseudoAI is designed to submit the minimized or pseudonymized version rather than the original values. Processing by the external AI provider is governed by the agreement, account configuration and privacy terms applicable to that provider.

**PseudoAI personnel.** Authorised PseudoAI personnel may receive limited information where necessary to provide support, maintenance, security or incident response requested by the customer.

**Service providers.** Where PseudoAI uses third-party infrastructure or service providers to operate parts of its own services, such providers may process only the information necessary to provide those services and must be subject to appropriate contractual and security obligations.

A current list of relevant service providers and subprocessors used by PseudoAI is available at **https://pseudoai.nl/dienstverleners**.

In the standard organisation-controlled deployment, these service providers do not receive original customer documents, prompts, token mappings or re-identification keys as part of PseudoAI’s normal detection and pseudonymization process.

## 8. OpenAI and ChatGPT

PseudoAI is an independent service and is not operated by OpenAI.

When a user intentionally submits minimized or pseudonymized information to ChatGPT, that information becomes subject to the terms, privacy practices and account configuration applicable to the user’s OpenAI service.

PseudoAI does not determine how OpenAI processes information once information has been submitted to OpenAI.

Users and organisations should therefore ensure that their use of ChatGPT is compatible with their own privacy, confidentiality and information-security requirements.

## 9. Legal basis

Where PseudoAI processes personal data on behalf of an organisation, the organisation determines the applicable legal basis for that processing and PseudoAI acts in accordance with the organisation’s documented instructions where it acts as a processor.

Where PseudoAI independently processes limited personal data necessary to manage its own customer relationship, security, support or legal obligations, processing may be based, as applicable, on performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation or PseudoAI’s legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service.

## 11. Security

PseudoAI is designed to support appropriate technical and organisational security measures.

Depending on the deployment, these measures may include:

- encryption in transit;
- role-based access control;
- access management;
- logging;
- monitoring;
- secrets management;
- patch management;
- vulnerability management;
- incident-response procedures;
- audit capabilities.

Access by PseudoAI personnel to personal data within a customer environment is not part of normal Service operation. Where such access is exceptionally required for support, it is limited to authorised personnel subject to confidentiality obligations.

No technical or organisational measure can provide absolute security.

## 12. International transfers

In the standard organisation-controlled deployment, the organisation determines where its PseudoAI environment and the information processed within it are hosted.

PseudoAI does not intentionally transfer original customer content outside that environment as part of normal detection and pseudonymization.

If information is intentionally submitted to an external AI provider, the geographic location and legal basis for processing or international transfer by that provider are governed by the customer’s arrangement with that provider.

Where PseudoAI itself transfers personal data outside the European Economic Area, PseudoAI will use an applicable lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards where required by law.

## 13. Data protection roles and contractual arrangements

For personal data contained in documents, prompts or other customer content, the organisation using PseudoAI generally determines the purposes and means of processing and acts as the data controller.

PseudoAI provides software that supports the organisation’s processing activities.

Where PseudoAI obtains access to personal data at the organisation’s request for support or maintenance purposes and processes that information on the organisation’s behalf, PseudoAI acts as a processor within the meaning of Article 28 GDPR for those activities.

Where an organisation has entered into a **PseudoAI Privacy and Data Processing** **Addendum**, Data Processing Agreement or other written processing arrangement with PseudoAI, that agreement provides additional terms governing the processing of personal data on behalf of that organisation.

If such an agreement conflicts with this Privacy Policy regarding processing carried out on behalf of the organisation, the separately agreed processing arrangement will prevail to the extent of that conflict.

## 14. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable data-protection law, individuals may have rights including access to their personal data, rectification of inaccurate information, erasure, restriction of processing, objection to certain processing and data portability.

Where personal data is processed by an organisation using PseudoAI, requests concerning that data should normally be directed to that organisation as data controller.

Where PseudoAI acts as controller for the relevant personal data, requests may be submitted to **privacy@pseudoai.nl**.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

Individuals in the European Economic Area also have the right to lodge a complaint with their competent supervisory authority. In the Netherlands, this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

## 15. Security incidents

Where PseudoAI becomes aware of a personal-data breach concerning personal data that PseudoAI processes on behalf of an organisation, PseudoAI will notify the relevant organisation without undue delay in accordance with applicable contractual and legal obligations.

The organisation remains responsible for determining whether notification to a supervisory authority or affected individuals is required where it acts as controller.

## 16. Children

Organisations using PseudoAI are responsible for determining whether their intended use involving minors is lawful and appropriate.
